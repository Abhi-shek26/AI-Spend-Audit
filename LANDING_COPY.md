## FAQ
### 1. What does this tool actually do?
It reviews your AI tool stack, estimates monthly waste, and recommends downgrades or consolidation opportunities with clear savings estimates.

### 2. Who is it for?
It is for finance, operations, and founders at small to mid-sized companies who own software spend and need a fast way to cut AI costs.

### 3. How accurate are the savings estimates?
They are directional and conservative. The tool uses rule-based estimates so you can spot obvious waste quickly, then validate the numbers against your own invoices and plans.

### 4. Do you store our data?
Audit results can be stored for sharing and follow-up. Sensitive data should be reviewed against your deployment settings and your own privacy policy.

### 5. How fast can we get a result?
Usually in under 2 minutes, depending on how many tools you enter and whether you want the AI summary.

## Extra landing-page notes
- Keep the promise narrow and concrete.
- Avoid generic phrases like "optimize workflows" unless you explain the specific outcome.
- Make the CTA match the product moment: audit, benchmark, or consultation.

Suggested supporting line for the page: "See what you can cut this quarter without guessing."
