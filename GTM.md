# GTM Plan

## Target user
**Primary ICP:** Finance or operations leaders at 20-200 person B2B SaaS companies, especially Heads of Finance, Finance Managers, RevOps leads, and founders who own spend.

This should stay narrow. The best-fit buyer is someone responsible for tool spend, vendor renewals, and budget conversations.

## Right-before-moment
List what they are doing or searching for right before they need this tool.
- Google searches:
  - "how to reduce SaaS spend without hurting productivity"
  - "software vendor audit template"
- Content they scroll:
  - CFO newsletters, SaaS operator posts on X, and Reddit threads about tool sprawl and budget cuts
- Trigger events:
  - Renewal season or budget reforecast
  - Leadership asks for a quick cost-cutting plan without layoffs

## Where they hang out
Be specific and only include places you would actually post or lurk.
- Subreddits: r/SaaS, r/startups, r/Entrepreneur
- Slack groups: Pavilion, RevGenius, Indie Hackers, CMO / RevOps peer groups
- Discord servers: Build in Public communities, startup founder servers, product-led growth servers
- X lists / creators: SaaS CFOs, RevOps operators, startup finance creators, operator-focused X lists
- Founder communities: Indie Hackers, YC Bookface-style communities, local startup meetups

## First 100 users in 30 days with $0 paid budget
Break this into concrete daily actions.
1. Days 1-3: DM 20 founders and finance leads from warm intro lists and public X replies. Send a 2-sentence note with one screenshot showing the audit output and one-line offer to run their stack for free.
2. Days 4-10: Post in 3 niche communities with a before/after demo, then follow up in-thread with a short breakdown of savings logic. DM anyone who engages and offer a live audit call.
3. Days 11-20: Ask early responders for 2 intros each to similar finance/ops leads. Send personalized follow-ups with their company type and a sample benchmark report.
4. Days 21-30: Repost one strong example result, reply to relevant budget-cutting discussions on X and in Slack, and convert interested people with a simple booking link and a one-page PDF summary.

Be specific about:
- Who gets a DM: finance/ops leaders, founders, and RevOps owners at seed to Series A/B companies
- What message they receive: "I built a 2-minute audit that shows where AI tool spend is leaking and what you could cut this week. Want me to run yours?"
- What proof or screenshot you share: a redacted results page with top savings, benchmark comparison, and a sample recommendation
- What follow-up cadence you use: day 2, day 5, and day 10 follow-ups only, then stop if no reply

## Unfair distribution channel
What is the one thing only you or Credex could do?
- Credex can turn the audit into a direct pathway to a consultation and credit purchase, so the product itself acts like a lead qualifier and sales assistant.
- Why it is unfair:
  - Most tools can surface savings, but Credex can connect the result to a live buying path, internal expertise, and a concrete credit-based offer.

## Week-1 traction definition
If this works, what numbers prove it?
- Visits: 250
- Audit starts: 80
- Completed audits: 35
- Credex consultation bookings: 8
- Conversion to booked calls: 20-25% of completed audits

## Positioning note
Avoid generic SaaS language. Say exactly what problem you solve, for whom, and why now.

Draft positioning: "For finance and ops leaders at fast-growing SaaS companies, this tool shows which AI subscriptions are wasting money and which can be consolidated, so they can cut spend before renewals hit."
