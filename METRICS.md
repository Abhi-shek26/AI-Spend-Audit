# Metrics

## North Star metric
**North Star:** Completed audits that lead to a Credex consultation booking.

Why this metric:
- The product is a lead-generation tool, not a daily habit app.
- A completed audit alone is valuable, but the business outcome is a booked consultation.
- This metric connects product usage to revenue.

## Input metrics
1. **Audit start rate**
   - How many visitors begin the audit form.
2. **Audit completion rate**
   - How many started audits become completed audits.
3. **Booking rate from completed audits**
   - How many completed audits turn into Credex consultation bookings.

## What to instrument first
- Landing page view
- Audit form start
- Audit form completion
- Results page view
- Share click
- Consultation booking click
- Credit purchase event (if available)

## Pivot threshold
Pivot if, after initial traffic:
- Audit completion rate is below 25% or
- Booked consultation rate from completed audits is below 10% or
- The average value per booked consultation does not exceed CAC by at least 3x.

## Notes
Use these metrics as a simple funnel:
`visit -> audit start -> audit completion -> consultation booking -> credit purchase`

Avoid vanity metrics like raw page views unless they improve one of the funnel steps above.

## Suggested baseline targets
- Landing page visit to audit start: 30%
- Audit start to audit completion: 50%
- Audit completion to consultation booking: 20%
- Consultation booking to credit purchase: 25-30%

If those baseline targets hold, the product is healthy enough to justify more distribution investment.
