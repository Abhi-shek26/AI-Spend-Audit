## Outreach Strategy by Audience

### Engineers (Individual Contributors)
- Angle: Personal productivity + budget control
- Channels: Twitter, Reddit (r/cscareerquestions, r/devops), Dev.to
- Message: "Check your spending, might surprise you"

### Engineering Managers
- Angle: Budget optimization, team efficiency
- Channels: Email, LinkedIn, Slack communities (e.g., Rands Leadership Slack)
- Message: "Know your team's AI spending? Here's how to optimize it"

### Founders / CTOs
- Angle: Burn rate reduction, operational excellence
- Channels: Email, LinkedIn, Twitter, Startup communities
- Message: "One quick fix in your monthly burn"

### Finance / Accounting
- Angle: Visibility, reporting, vendor consolidation
- Channels: Email, LinkedIn Finance groups
- Message: "Get visibility and control over tech tool spend"

### Communities & Newsletters
- Channels: Tech newsletters, Slack communities, Discord servers
- Approach: Sponsorships + free distribution

## Metrics to Track

**For each outreach channel:**
- Link CTR (click-through rate)
- Audit completion rate
- Share rate (referrals generated)
- Implementation rate (conversion to paying/engaged users)
- NPS / feedback

---

*Tips for success:*
- **Personalize** when possible (mention their specific role/challenge)
- **Urgency** without pressure ("1-minute check" or "quick data point")
- **Benefit-forward** (focus on savings, not your product)
- **Low friction** (free, no sign-up, fast)
- **Shareability** (encourage them to send to others)
